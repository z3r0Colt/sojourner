roads.geojson

The Roman roads in this file are derived from the geodata of the Ancient World
Mapping Center, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill
(https://github.com/AWMC/geodata), made available under the Open Database
License (ODbL) v1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

This file, as a derivative of that database, is likewise made available under
the Open Database License v1.0. It was made by tools/build-atlas-roads.py in
the Sojourner source: clipped to the biblical world, simplified, and reduced
to each road's name, rank, and whether its course is known.

The Old Testament routes in the same file (the Way of the Sea, the King's
Highway, the Way of the Patriarchs, the Way of Shur, the Jericho road) are
drawn through the traditional stations using OpenBible.info's coordinates
(CC BY 4.0); their courses between stations are approximate.
